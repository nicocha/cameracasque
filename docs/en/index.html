Your Cambox is back in action.

Cambox closed and its app left the App Store and Google Play. Caméra Casque takes over: the free app for live preview, recording, gallery and settings of your Cambox V3+ or V4, on iPhone and Android. On horseback, motorbike, bike or in the car.

Why this app exists

  1. The official app is gone

    With Cambox closed, the app can no longer be downloaded, and new phones can’t install it anymore.

  2. Your camera still works

    Your Cambox V3+ or V4 still films. All it lacked was a way to control it from your phone.

  3. Caméra Casque takes over

    An independent app, compatible with Cambox V3+ and V4, rewritten for iPhone and Android and released as open source to last.

Features

Everything the Cambox app did.
Made simpler.

Frame it live.

See exactly what the camera films while you adjust its angle, under the visor or on the riding helmet. Start a video or take a photo from the phone, with haptic feedback on every tap.

  • 16:9 frame for video, full image for photos (V3+)
  • Recording timer
  • Detects a recording started from the camera’s button

Your videos, just like in Photos.

Browse the SD card in a familiar grid that shows the visible period. Swipe from one video to the next, then save, share or delete: one item or a whole selection.

  • Save straight to Photos
  • Duration, size and position of each file
  • Multiple selection and bulk delete

Every setting in its place.

Resolution, stabilization, file duration, WDR, exposure, white balance, WiFi name and password. The app recognizes your model, reads the choices it offers and only shows the ones it accepts.

  • Battery level and firmware version
  • Camera clock set automatically
  • Card formatting and reset

The manual in your pocket.

Buttons, lights, connection, mounting, charging, troubleshooting: the essentials of the user manual are built into the app and stay readable offline.

  • Available even with the camera off
  • Light and dark mode
  • No data sent to the internet

Install

Install Caméra Casque.
Ready in two minutes.

iPhone

Through TestFlight, Apple’s app for versions distributed outside the App Store. iOS 16.4 or later. Compatible with Cambox V3+ and V4.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the invitation link below on your iPhone.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.
Open the TestFlight invitation

Updates arrive automatically through TestFlight.

Android

APK file published on GitHub. Android 8 or later. Compatible with Cambox V3+ and V4.

  1. Download the APK with the button below.
  2. Open it, then allow your browser to install apps if Android asks.
  3. Tap Install.
Download the APK

Previous versions remain available on GitHub.

Then connect the camera

  1. Turn it onHold the On/Off button for 3 seconds. It vibrates: it is on pause.
  2. Turn on WiFiShort press on the WiFi button. The orange light blinks fast.
  3. Join its networkIn the phone’s WiFi settings: CAMBOXV3PLUS (V3+) or CAMBOX (V4), password 12345678.
  4. Open the appIt recognizes your camera model. The preview appears.

The phone says this network has no internet access? Choose to stay connected. On Android, turn off mobile data if the app doesn’t find the camera. Buttons and lights described here: Cambox V3+; for the V4, see its manual.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Where can I download the Cambox V3+ or V4 app?

The official app is no longer available since Cambox shut down. Caméra Casque replaces it: free, on iPhone and Android, and compatible with the Cambox V3+, V4, V4 Plus and V4 Pro. See Install.

The Cambox app no longer works, what should I do?

Install Caméra Casque instead. Nothing needs to be configured on the camera: join its WiFi network as before, and you get the live preview, your videos and your settings back.

Is Caméra Casque the official Cambox app?

No. It is an independent project, made by a Cambox owner for the community, with no link to Cambox Vision. It was written from scratch from the camera’s WiFi protocol.

Is my Cambox V4 compatible?

Yes. Caméra Casque automatically recognizes the Cambox V3+ and the Cambox V4, V4 Plus and V4 Pro: live preview, recording, gallery and settings (up to 4K on the V4 Plus and V4 Pro, stabilization, white balance…). The V4 only shoots video: photo mode only appears for the V3+. The Cambox V3, without the “+”, has not been tested.

Why does the live preview pause during recording?

While it films, the camera keeps its image for the recording. The V3+ then only streams it in RTSP over UDP, a format iOS does not allow without a special Apple permission; the V4 turns its preview off to record. The app therefore shows the preview before and after recording, and a timer during. Frame it, then start.

Does the app collect my data?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics. The app only talks to your camera, on the camera’s own WiFi network. It asks for Photos access only when you save a video.

Can I update the camera firmware?

No, and you don’t need to: no update was ever released and the app works with the camera’s original firmware. Don’t install firmware from another camera, you could make it unusable.

Why TestFlight on iPhone?

An app that controls a product whose brand has disappeared cannot be published on the App Store by an individual. TestFlight, Apple’s testing service, makes it simple to distribute and keep up to date.

I found a bug or a feature is missing.

Open an issue on GitHub. The code is open: contributions are welcome.
