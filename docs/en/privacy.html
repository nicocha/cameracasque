Caméra Casque for Cambox V3+ and V4

Privacy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

In short

Caméra Casque, the app compatible with Cambox V3+ and V4, does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Network communication

The app only communicates with your Cambox camera, on the local WiFi network the camera creates (address 192.168.1.254 for the V3+, 192.168.2.1 for the V4). It sends no request to the internet or to any third-party server.

Photos and videos

When you tap “Save”, the app downloads the video or photo from the camera and adds it to your phone’s gallery. For this it asks for add-only access: it does not read or browse your existing photos.

When you tap “Share”, the file is prepared in a temporary folder of the app, then handed to the app you choose. It is deleted afterwards.

Permissions requested

  • Local network (iPhone): connect to the camera.
  • Add to Photos / to the gallery: save the camera’s files, only when you ask.

Children

The app collects no data, including from children.

Source code

The app’s code is public: anyone can check what it does. See the source code.
