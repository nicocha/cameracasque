Caméra Casque for Cambox V3+
FR

Privacy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Summary

Caméra Casque, the app compatible with the Cambox V3+, does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no account system, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Network communication

The app communicates only with your Cambox camera on the local WiFi network it creates (address 192.168.1.254). It sends no requests to the internet or to any third-party server.

Photos and videos

When you tap “Save”, the app downloads the video or photo from the camera and adds it to your phone’s photo library. To do this, it requests “Add only” access: it does not read or browse your existing photos.

When you tap “Share”, the file is prepared in a temporary app folder and passed to the app you choose. It is deleted afterward.

Permissions requested

  • Local network (iPhone): connect to the camera.
  • Add to Photos / Gallery: save files from the camera only when you ask for it.

Children

The app collects no data, including from children.

Source code

The app code is public: anyone can verify what it does. View the source code.
